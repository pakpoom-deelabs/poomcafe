RITUAL

The Coffee
Ritual

Every cup at Poomcafe follows a path we've refined over thousands of brews — a quiet ceremony you can taste in every sip.

01

Source

We work directly with small farms in Chiang Rai, Doi Chang, and Lampang. Each harvest is a relationship built over years.

02

Roast

Roasted in small batches every Tuesday. We chase sweetness, not darkness — light enough to let origin speak clearly.

03

Brew

Every method is considered. Pour-over, espresso, cold — we match the bean to the process that honours it most.

04

Serve

In your hands — warm, intentional, made exactly for this moment. That's the only way we know how to do it.
